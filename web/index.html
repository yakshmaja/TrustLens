How it works

Four independent sources, one honest read.

Each source is checked separately. A missing page or a private repo is treated as missing evidence, never as proof that something is wrong.

What the score means

It measures how strong and consistent the evidence is, not whether a company is "good". A low score needs concrete contradictions; missing data alone can't cause one.

  1. 0–34Serious concerns
  2. 35–54Limited
  3. 55–74Moderate
  4. 75–89Strong
  5. 90+Very strong
