TrustLens Company due diligence

Check a company before you work with it.

TrustLens gathers public evidence about a company (domain records, its website, GitHub activity and independent web coverage) and reports how well that evidence holds together.

A check takes about one to two minutes. Try or .

What gets checked

Domain records
Registration date, registrar and name servers from WHOIS.
Company website
Homepage content, about, team and contact pages, social links.
Engineering activity
The matching GitHub organization and its public repositories.
Web coverage
Funding, leadership, reviews and news from independent sources.
